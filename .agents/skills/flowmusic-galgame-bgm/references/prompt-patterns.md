# Prompt Patterns

## General dialogue loop

```text
Japanese galgame background music for a normal dialogue scene. [Scene/mood]. [2–4 instruments]. [Tempo]. Functional dialogue background music, quiet and unobtrusive under long conversations. Simple repeating motif, low melodic density, stable tempo, stable dynamics, static arrangement, minimal harmonic development, consistent instrumentation and energy throughout. Designed for a 2–4 minute seamless loop. Instrumental only, no vocals. No climax, no buildup, no breakdown, no dramatic transitions, no key changes, no orchestral swell, no sudden percussion, no intro or outro, no big ending, no strong ending cadence.
```

## Quiet night / sea / lighthouse room

```text
Japanese galgame background music for a normal dialogue scene, quiet night by the sea inside a cozy lighthouse room. Soft piano, gentle ambient pad, subtle acoustic guitar, very light bass. Calm, intimate, slightly wistful, warm and unobtrusive. 2–4 minute seamless loop, simple repeating motif, stable tempo, stable dynamics, low melodic density, minimal harmonic development, consistent mood and instrumentation throughout. Designed to sit quietly under long character conversations. Instrumental only, no vocals. No climax, no buildup, no dramatic transitions, no orchestral swell, no sudden percussion, no strong drum hits, no breakdown, no key changes, no intro or outro, no big ending, no strong ending cadence.
```

## Playful character dialogue

```text
Japanese galgame character dialogue BGM. Piano, pizzicato strings, and very light percussion. Cute, mischievous, witty, slightly teasing, but restrained enough to stay behind dialogue. Moderate tempo. Short repeating melodic motif, simple accompaniment, low musical complexity, stable dynamics, consistent playful mood throughout, seamless loop. Instrumental only, no vocals. No climax, no buildup, no dramatic orchestration, no modulation, no breakdown, no large transitions, no flashy drum fills, no intro or outro, no big ending.
```

## Quiet emotional dialogue without melodrama

```text
Japanese visual novel quiet dialogue BGM. Soft piano and a very light ambient pad. Calm, intimate, slightly wistful, emotionally restrained. Slow tempo. Sparse repeating piano motif, limited chord progression, static arrangement, extremely gentle dynamics, minimal development, seamless loop, designed to remain unobtrusive under dialogue. Instrumental only, no vocals. No emotional climax, no crescendo, no dramatic chord progression, no modulation, no orchestral swell, no sudden changes, no intro or outro, no big ending.
```

## Repair patterns

If the result becomes too dramatic:
- add `functional dialogue background music`, `static arrangement`, `minimal development`, `stable dynamics`, `consistent energy throughout`;
- remove adjectives such as `cinematic`, `epic`, `powerful`, `emotional`, `evolving`;
- reduce the number of instruments;
- explicitly forbid `climax`, `buildup`, `orchestral swell`, `modulation`, and `large dynamic changes`.

If drums become intrusive:
- replace generic `percussion` with `very light restrained percussion` or remove percussion entirely;
- add `no sudden percussion`, `no strong drum hits`, `no drum fills`.

If the loop seam is obvious:
- add `no intro or outro`, `no big ending`, `no strong ending cadence`, `consistent arrangement from beginning to end`, `seamless loop`;
- avoid prompts that imply a narrative arc.

If the music is too busy:
- use only 2–3 instruments;
- add `low melodic density`, `simple repeating motif`, `simple accompaniment`, `low musical complexity`;
- remove excess scene/worldbuilding descriptors.
